## 🔌 CRITICAL METHODS TO UNDERSTAND

```
1. VOIPCallManager.startOutgoingVoipCall()
   └─→ Creates: UUID callId
   └─→ Calls: FirebaseSignalingManager.createCall()
   └─→ Launches: CallInProgressActivity

2. IncomingCallListener.start()
   └─→ Watches: Firestore for status="ringing"
   └─→ On change: Shows notification + launches Activity

3. WebRTCClient.initialize()
   └─→ Creates: PeerConnectionFactory
   └─→ Creates: JavaAudioDeviceModule

4. WebRTCClient.createPeerConnection()
   └─→ Creates: Peer connection
   └─→ Adds: Observer for ICE, Track, Signaling

5. FirebaseSignalingManager.listenToCall()
   └─→ Listens: For offer_sdp, answer_sdp, status
   └─→ Calls: onOffer, onAnswer, onStatus callbacks

6. WebRTCClient.startDeepfakeDetection()
   └─→ Creates: DeepfakeDetectionService
   └─→ Calls: startMonitoring()
   └─→ Calls: attachIncomingDetectionSink()

7. DeepfakeDetectionService.startMonitoring()
   └─→ Starts: Background loop
   └─→ Accumulates: 3-second audio buffer
   └─→ Calls: modelRunner.predict()
   └─→ Saves: detectionDao.insert()

8. CallInProgressActivity.onDestroy()
   └─→ Calls: signaling.stopListening()
   └─→ Calls: ActiveCallStore.clear()
   └─→ Calls: IncomingCallListener.start()
```
